## Root cause corrected

Run #1301 had 461 passed and 2 failed in the complete-checkout full-suite job. Both failures traced to `measurement_310/command_intent.py` treating the journal tail as valid only when it ended with the two-byte text sequence backslash+n rather than the single newline byte.

Commit `10d0881f6a1fb9542e696e6ef50823e9c335b2c3` changes the check to `raw.endswith(b"\\n")`. The persisted-history scan can now read a normal newline-terminated journal and enforce command-ID uniqueness after construction of a new journal object.

The full suite was rerun on that exact commit and passed. This confirms the two host-suite failures are corrected in the candidate checkout; it does not establish firmware/wire identity or physical safety.
